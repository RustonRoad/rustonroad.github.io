Women's Motorcycle Helmets

Any helmet sold for road use in the UK must carry ECE 22.05 or the newer ECE 22.06 approval. 22.06 adds tests for rotational impact and visors, so prefer it on a new purchase.

SHARP, the UK Government helmet scheme, crash-tests helmets and scores them from one to five stars. A five-star SHARP rating on a mid-price helmet is real value.

Full face helmets protect the chin and are quieter at speed; open face jet helmets suit scooters, retro bikes and town riding. Measure around your head just above the eyebrows and use the maker's chart. A new helmet should feel snug with no pressure points and should not roll forward when you push the chin.
