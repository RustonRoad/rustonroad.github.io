Women's Motorcycle Boots

Proper riding boots are tested to EN 13634, which rates height, abrasion, impact cut and transverse rigidity. Look for reinforced heel and toe, ankle cups on both sides and a gear-shift pad.

Short urban riding shoes are easy to walk in and fine for town; mid-calf touring boots protect the shin and handle long days and rain. Classic leather side-zip boots sit in between and look at home off the bike.

Fit them with riding socks on, and check you can feel the gear lever through the sole. Fast closures such as dial or ATOP systems make boots quicker to get on and off at every stop.
