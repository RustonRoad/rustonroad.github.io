Women's Motorcycle Gloves

Riding gloves are tested to EN 13594. Level 1 suits everyday road use, level 2 is the higher grade, and a KP mark means the knuckle protection itself passed impact tests.

In summer, short perforated or hard-knuckle gloves keep hands cool. For UK winters, look for a thermal lining such as Thinsulate, a waterproof layer and long gauntlet cuffs that go over the jacket sleeve.

Gloves should be snug with no loose fabric at the fingertips when you grip the bar. A wrist strap stops them coming off in a slide, so make sure it closes firmly.
