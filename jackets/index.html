Women's Motorcycle Jackets

Start with the label. Riding garments sold in the UK and EU are tested to EN 17092, rated AAA, AA, A, B or C. AAA is the most abrasion and impact resistant; A suits everyday road riding. A fashion leather jacket with no rating is a style piece, not protection.

Leather gives the best slide resistance and moulds to you over time. Textile wins on waterproofing, vents and price, which makes it the practical pick for UK weather and commuting.

Check the armour pockets. CE level 1 or 2 shoulder and elbow armour should come fitted; a back protector pocket lets you add one later. Try the jacket in a riding position: sleeves should still cover your wrists with arms stretched to the bars, and the armour should sit on the joint.
