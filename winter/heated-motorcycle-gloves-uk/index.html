Yes. The Amazon UK listing includes two 3000mAh rechargeable batteries and a model-specific cable to run the gloves from a 12V motorcycle supply, with three heat levels for each of the palm and back-of-hand zones.

Do I have to wire them to my bike?

No. You can use them on the included batteries only. The 12V connection is there for longer rides; when connected, the listing says the gloves heat and recharge the batteries at the same time.

Will running them from 12V flatten my battery?

Any heated kit adds electrical load. Check your bike's charging output, connect through a fused supply (ideally ignition-switched) and fall back to the batteries if your bike has little spare capacity.
